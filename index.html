ViceCityScreams Uploader

A small personal tool for the YouTube channel ViceCityScreams.

What it does

ViceCityScreams Uploader runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads short gaming clips (YouTube Shorts) that the channel owner has edited and approved to the owner's own YouTube channel, using the official YouTube Data API. Every video is reviewed and approved manually before it is uploaded.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. There are no other users, no sign-up and no public access.

Data

The tool does not collect, sell or share any user data. See the Privacy Policy.
